AZYRITH: Idle Legends - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21  |  Developer: Vexhollow Games  |  Contact: vexhollowgames@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the mobile game AZYRITH: Idle Legends ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (heroes, items, currencies, settings such as volume, and tutorial progress) is saved in a file on your device. You do not need to create an account to play. Uninstalling the game removes this file unless you use cloud save (see section 3).

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.
  • The game does not access your camera, microphone, photos or files.

3. Sign-in and cloud save (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the game with your Play Games display name and a player identifier so the game can show who is signed in and, where enabled, save your progress to Google's servers. This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

4. Advertising

The game may show optional rewarded video ads (for example to double a reward). To show ads, our advertising partners (Unity LevelPlay, which shows ads from networks such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads) may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and ad interaction data, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation of what you bought so the game can grant it. This includes the Premium Gems packs; Premium Gems are only ever granted after Google Play confirms a payment.

6. Analytics and crash reports

The game may use analytics and crash-reporting services (for example Firebase) to understand how the game is used and to fix problems. They may collect device information, app version, crash logs and a random installation identifier. They do not collect your name or contact details.

7. How we use information

  • to run the game, save and restore your progress and grant purchases;
  • to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to find and fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention, deletion and security

Local progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. To ask us to delete any data we hold about you, or to ask a question about it, write to vexhollowgames@gmail.com. Data held by Google, advertising partners or analytics providers is deleted according to their own policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Third-party services

  • Google Play services and Google Play Games: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation for rewarded video ads, by Unity / ironSource): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Unity Ads and ironSource Ads (ad networks served through LevelPlay): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob (an ad network served through LevelPlay): policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase (analytics and crash reports, if enabled): firebase.google.com/support/privacy

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Continuing to play after an update means you accept it.
